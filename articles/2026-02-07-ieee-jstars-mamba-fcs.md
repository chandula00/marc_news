Proud to share that our Remote Sensing research group at the Multidisciplinary AI Research Centre, University of Peradeniya, has received an acceptance for publication in **IEEE JSTARS** (Q1, SCIE; Impact Factor: 5.3).

Our work, **"Mamba-FCS: Joint Spatio-Frequency Feature Fusion, Change-Guided Attention, and SeK Inspired Loss for Enhanced Semantic Change Detection in Remote Sensing,"** advances **computer vision for remote sensing** toward more reliable **AI-powered systems**. Semantic change detection is vital for mapping how environments evolve and for real-world decision-making, including **disaster management**, **precision agriculture**, **urban growth monitoring**, and broader environmental change analysis.

In our experiments, the proposed method **outperforms recent state-of-the-art approaches**, demonstrating strong potential for practical deployment. This achievement is especially meaningful to us as it emerged from one of our **final-year undergraduate projects**, carried out entirely within a **University of Peradeniya–affiliated team**.
