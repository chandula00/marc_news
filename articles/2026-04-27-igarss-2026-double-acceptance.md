We are absolutely thrilled to share that two research papers from the Multidisciplinary AI Research Centre (MARC) have been officially accepted for IGARSS 2026 in Washington, D.C.

It's incredibly inspiring to see our teams pushing the boundaries of AI and remote sensing on a global stage. A huge congratulations to the brilliant authors Nichula Sathmith, Dineth Perera, Oshadha Samarakoon, Buddhi Wijenayake, Prof. Roshan Godaliyadda, Prof. Vijitha Herath, Prof. Mpb Ekanayake, Prof. Vishal Patel for this remarkable achievement.

- **A Controlled Benchmark of Visual State-Space Backbones** – Exploring the frontiers of Mamba-based backbones for remote-sensing segmentation!
- **Mitigating Long-Tail Bias via Prompt-Controlled Diffusion Augmentation** – Innovating minority-class segmentation with diffusion models, in collaboration with Johns Hopkins University.
