We are incredibly excited to announce that MARC has officially become a member of the Artificial Intelligence Capacity Development Network (AICDN), proudly representing Sri Lanka on the global stage!

With global AI capacity often fragmented and unevenly distributed, the AICDN is dedicated to bridging the AI divide. The network facilitates global coordination and mobilizes vital in-kind contributions from universities, corporations, and Member States.

As a member, MARC will actively support capacity-building activities and collaborate on shared normative commitments to ensure AI development is inclusive and accessible.

This is a massive step forward for Sri Lanka's AI ecosystem, and we are thrilled to contribute to shaping a better, more connected AI future for everyone!
